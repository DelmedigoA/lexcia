About Lexcia

Make the knowledge you already have easier to reach.

Why Lexcia

The information exists. It isn’t always accessible.

Organizations invest years of work, money and resources in creating valuable content and knowledge, yet much of it stays buried inside their websites. Lexcia closes the gap between the knowledge an organization holds and its visitors’ ability to actually reach and use it.

What we offer

Not a generic chatbot.

Lexcia is a fully customizable, AI-powered conversational layer for your website, from the engine behind it to the way it looks, speaks and behaves. We adapt the entire experience to your organization.

Engine

Retrieval, knowledge structure, model behavior, tools and logic.

Interface

Content

How it works

From the content you have to a conversation on your site.

  1. Connect your content

    We start from the pages, documents and knowledge your website already holds, and how visitors move through them.

  2. Tailor the experience

    Engine, interface, design and behavior are shaped around your organization, its brand and the way it speaks.

  3. Embed it in your site

    Visitors ask in their own words and get where they need to go, with your page still in view.

  4. Keep improving

    Hosting, monitoring, maintenance, analytics and ongoing improvements are part of the subscription.
