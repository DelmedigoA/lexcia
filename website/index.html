About Lexcia

The information exists. It isn’t always accessible.

Organizations invest years of work, money and resources in creating valuable content and knowledge, yet much of it stays buried inside their websites.

Lexcia closes the gap between the knowledge an organization holds and its visitors’ ability to reach and use it, through a natural conversation that keeps the page in view.
